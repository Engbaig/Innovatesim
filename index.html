About InnovateSim

We build the environments where clinical skills are earned safely.

InnovateSim Technologies Inc. designs healthcare simulation products and advises hospitals, nursing and medical schools, and training centers on building the facilities that house them. From individual manikins to full-scale simulation centers, we cover the complete lifecycle: concept, design, installation, and ongoing support.

Our teams bring together simulation engineering, clinical education, and facility planning under one roof — so clients deal with a single partner instead of stitching vendors together themselves.

  • 01 Supplying simulation products with trusted service, and helping you incorporate them into your training programs
  • 02 Simulation facility and technical infrastructure design
  • 03 Installation, commissioning and activation
  • 04 User training, accreditation guidance and lifecycle support
